The core technique, in four lines

1 · Empty

Close the eyes. Don’t pay willing attention to thoughts, and don’t start any of your own (p.95).

2 · One idea

Hold a single idea or image-less “orientation”. Don’t paint a picture; don’t add meanings (p.99).

3 · Let thoughts pass

Don’t reject them. Rejection echoes. Walk toward the park and ignore the shop windows (p.100).

4 · Return gently

Close slowly; walk quietly; speak softly. The state should enter ordinary waking life (p.101).
